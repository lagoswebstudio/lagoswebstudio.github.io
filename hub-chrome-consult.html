02 / The studio

Designed around real life.

HUB CHROME CONSULT is an interior design and home décor business based in Gbagada, Lagos. The aim is simple: make spaces feel more considered, more comfortable and more personal.

Rather than decorating for a passing trend, we look at how a room is used, the mood it should create and the details that bring everything together.
